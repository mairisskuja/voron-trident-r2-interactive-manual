## p.291 — PANELS & FINISHING TOUCHES
Shows: Chapter title page. Colour render of the finished printer: tinted panels and doors, spool holder at the top right, PTFE tube. Not a step.

## p.292 — PANELS
Shows: View of the top rear extrusion from behind with 2 M5 T-nuts (light blue) in its rear slot.
Hardware labels: "M5 T-Nut".
Tips:
- WHAT ARE THESE FOR?: These roll-in nuts are used later when installing the filament inlet and spool holder. It's easier to install them now, before the panel is in the way.

## p.293 — PANELS
Shows: The rear panel (notch at the top edge) with foam tape along all edges; 7 clips (corner and midspan shapes) with M3x12 SHCS; the same clips from behind with M3 hammerhead nuts on the screws.
Hardware labels: "Foam Tape 3mm", "M3x12 SHCS", "M3 Hammerhead Nut".
Tips:
- HAMMERHEAD NUTS?: A drop of threadlocker will turn the hammerhead nuts into a 1/4 turn quick release for the panels. Best done once the assembly is finished.
- APPLY FOAM TAPE: Use foam tape on the contact areas between the panels and the frame to mitigate noise from vibrations.

## p.302 — PANELS
Shows: Door latch: M3x8 SHCS pushed through; 2 magnets glued in; M3 hammerhead nut on the screw. Below: the two door handles side by side, 1 magnet each.

## p.312 — SPOOL HOLDER
Shows: PTFE tube slipped into the top channel of the spool holder arm; the arm (light blue) slid into the base.

- INSERT PTFE TUBE: Use a 86mm PTFE tube with a 2 mm inner diameter. Bend the tube slightly to slip it into the mounting holes.

## p.315 — SPOOL HOLDER
Shows: The PTFE tube (light blue) from the holder around the rear right corner into the filament inlet; close-ups of the clamp screws in the holder and the inlet.
Tips:
- INSTALL PTFE TUBE: Run the PTFE tube as one continuous piece from the bowden retainer to the toolhead, passing it through the inlet to hold it in place. Avoid sharp bends in the filament path. For the reverse bowden, use a PTFE tube with a 3mm inner and 4mm outer diameter. When tightening the screw that locks the tube in place, make sure not to crush it.
