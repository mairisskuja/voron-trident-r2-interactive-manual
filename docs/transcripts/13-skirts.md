# Voron Trident Assembly Manual: Skirts transcript, pp. 269–290

Source: `Assembly_Manual_Trident.pdf` (version 2026-06-24). Text in tips and labels is verbatim from `pdftotext -layout` (ligatures written out). Illustration descriptions are my own, from 1000 px renders (p.295 also at 2000 px). Conventions as in the pilot transcript: blue pill = hardware label, light-blue fill = parts added, red heart = accent part. "VORONDESIGN.COM" and page numbers are omitted.

From p.277 on, the printer is drawn **upside down** (skirts above, Z motors and electronics deck visible from below).

## p.271 — PREPARATION
Shows: Left: the accent front cover (light blue, red heart) slid into the LCD case. Right: the back of the case with 4 M3 heat set inserts in the corner bosses. Bottom: the front cover with a circled tab (the built-in support).
Hardware labels: "M3 Heat Set Insert".
Tips:
- FRONT COVER: The front cover is held in place by the heat set inserts. Hold the front face firmly in place while inserting the heat set inserts.
- BUILT-IN SUPPORT: Remove the highlighted section. It’s a built-in support for printability.

## p.273 — LCD

## p.274 — LCD
Shows: Left two: a light-blue ring threaded onto the encoder (two positions). Right: the knob (light blue) pressed onto the encoder.
Tips:
- OPTION: LIGHT BLOCKER: Some LCDs come with a smaller encoder knob. This extra piece prevents excess light bleed. Threads onto the encoder before the knob is pressed on.

## p.276 — SKIRTS

## p.288 — WIRING
Shows: Controller board drawing (M1–M8, E0/E1/E2 OUT, BED OUT/IN, PWR IN, FAN0–FAN2, TE0/TE1, EXP1/EXP2). "Electronics Fans" wired to FAN0 (− and +); "Mini12864 Display" ribbon cables to EXP1 and EXP2. Inset: the LCD back with the EXP sockets circled.
Tips:
- ELECTRONICS COOLING FAN: 2x 0.25mm²(AWG24) or larger
- WHICH IS WHICH?: The socket with 1 dot below it is EXP1; the socket with 2 dots is EXP2.
- WARNING: DISPLAY HOOKUP: Before connecting the display make sure to review the documentation on the VORON documentation site (https://voron.link/ypdmcb2). If you are using a “FYSETC mini12864” please review section 3.3 of the FYSETC Spider documentation (https://voron.link/m6etwnl).
