# X Axis: transcript, pp. 118–140

Source: `Assembly_Manual_Trident.pdf` (version 2026-06-24). Tip titles and texts are verbatim from `pdftotext -layout` (wraps joined, ligatures written out). Illustration descriptions are my own, from 1000 px renders. Conventions as in [the pilot transcript](../pilot-transcript.md): blue pill = hardware label, light-blue fill = parts added in the step, red heart = accent part, blue circle = attention callout.

Skipped: **p.118** (chapter title page), **p.141** (trivia: "The VORON Legacy is a modernized design true to the spirit of the original VORON 1.0.").

## p.119 — OVERVIEW
Shows: The finished X axis: the X extrusion with the X linear rail on its front face, the left XY joint (with its belt idler) at the left end, the right XY joint at the right end, and the two X carriage halves on the rail carriage in the middle.
Labels: X Carriage (×2), Right XY Joint, Left XY Joint, X Linear Rail.
Tips: none.

## p.120 — PART PREPARATION
Shows: Three groups. Left: a small flat plate (the probe bracket) with two M3 inserts. Middle: the two X carriage halves, each with two M3 inserts in the front face (one high, one low). Right: the right X carriage half again (ghosted), with two more inserts in its side face.
Hardware labels: M3 Heat Set Insert (×3 labels).
Tips: none.

## p.121 — PART PREPARATION
Shows: Two versions of the cable bridge (both accent parts, red heart). Left: three inserts above a 3-hole top. Right: two inserts above a 2-hole top.
Tips:
- GENERIC CABLE CHAINS: The 3 hole pattern is usually found on generic cable chains.
- IGUS CABLE CHAINS: IGUS chains have 2 mounting holes.
- WHICH TO CHOOSE?: Pick the style that matches the mounting pattern of your cable chains.

## p.122 — PART PREPARATION
Shows: Two XY joint halves, seen on their mounting face, each receiving 2 large M5 inserts and several M3 inserts.
Hardware labels: M5 Heat Set Insert (×2 labels), M3 Heat Set Insert (×2 labels).
Tips: none.

## p.123 — RIGHT XY JOINT
Shows: Left: a right XY joint half; a blue circle marks a small channel in a wall. Right: the upper half turned upside down, with an M3x8 SHCS going into it (compare p.129, where the upper half is drawn both ways up).
Hardware labels: M3x8 SHCS.
Tips:
- CABLE PATH: The printed parts for the right XY joint have a small channel to guide the endstop wires..
- LOCATING SCREW: This screw is used to align the upper and lower half of the XY joint.

## p.124 — RIGHT XY JOINT
Shows: Left: an M5x40 BHCS going down through the upper half (square pockets on top). Right: the upper half turned over, the screw shank up, with the stack shim – F695 – F695 – shim on it; the locating screw head is next to it.
Hardware labels: M5x40 BHCS, M5 Shim, F695 Bearing.
Tips:
- FLIP IT OVER: Insert the M5x40 screw, then flip the part over to install the bearing stack.

## p.125 — RIGHT XY JOINT
Shows: Left: the lower half (blue, with its slotted mounting plate) lowered onto the upturned upper half over the bearing stack; a blue circle marks the locating screw pocket. Right: the closed joint; a GT2 20 tooth idler beside it and an M5x40 BHCS coming down through the idler bore.
Hardware labels: M5x40 BHCS, GT2 20 Tooth Idler.
Tips:
- LOOSEY GOOSEY: The M5x40 screw should be snug but not tight - the idler must spin freely.
- MIND THE ORIENTATION: We are back to working with the upper half facing upwards.

## p.126 — RIGHT XY JOINT
Shows: Three accent plates: the plain one, the XY endstop PCB one, and the PCB one again with 2 M3 inserts.
Hardware labels: M3 Heat Set Insert.
Tips:
- PLAIN ACCENT PART: Use this XY joint accent part when running endstops on the X carriage and A motor mount, or when using sensorless homing.
- XY ENDSTOP PCB: Use this XY joint accent part when running an endstop PCB.
- RIGHT XY JOINT ACCENT PARTS: The right XY joint has multiple accent parts. Pick the one that matches your setup. The rest of the manual shows only the "plain" accent part.

## p.134 — X AXIS
Shows: The E extrusion three times, with the extrusion thumbnail (one short extrusion highlighted). Top: 2 M5 T-nuts at each end of the top slot (circled). Middle: 6 M3 T-nuts spread along the front slot. Bottom: 1 M5 T-nut at each end of the bottom slot (circled).
Hardware labels: M5 T-Nut (×4 labels), E Extrusion, M3 T-Nut.
Tips:
- T-NUT ORIENTATION: Insert the t-nuts as shown in the highlight.

## p.135 — X AXIS
Shows: Top: the MGN12 rail with its carriage (blue) laid on the front of the extrusion. Bottom: 6 M3x8 SHCS into the rail; a printed U-shaped guide (blue).
Hardware labels: MGN12 Rail, M3x8 SHCS.
Tips:
- MIND THE CARRIAGE: Temporarily secure the carriage with a piece of sticky tape to prevent it from sliding off the rail. Dropping the carriage likely irreparably damages it.
- CENTERED RAIL INSTALLATION GUIDE: Use the MGN12 guides to position the rail in the center of the extrusion prior to tightening the screws.

## p.139 — XY CABLE BRIDGE
Shows: Left: the 3-hole cable bridge (blue) set on the right XY joint. Right: 2 M5x16 BHCS through its foot.
Hardware labels: M5x16 BHCS (×2 labels).
Tips:
- USING AN UMBILICAL?: Skip the cable bridge if you're using an umbilical setup. Use M5x10 BHCS screws to attach the XY joint instead.

## p.140 — X AXIS SQUARING
Shows: Two top views of the printer: the gantry (blue) pushed fully back against the A and B drives; and at the front.
Tips:
- SQUARING THE GANTRY: Move the gantry all the way back until it hits the A and B drive on both sides. Fully tighten all screws on the X axis.
Other text: QR code, https://voron.link/cekh81l
