# Voron Trident Assembly Manual: transcript, pp. 49–76 (Y Axis)

Source: `Assembly_Manual_Trident.pdf` (VoronDesign, GPL-3.0, version 2026-06-24). Text in tips and labels is verbatim from `pdftotext -layout`, with line wraps joined and "ﬂ" written as "fl". The illustrations are described from 1000 px renders (p.59 and the pp.51–52 thumbnails also at 250–300 dpi). Conventions are as in [the pilot transcript](../pilot-transcript.md): blue pills are hardware labels, light-blue fill marks the parts added on the page, and the red heart marks an accent part.

Every page also carries "VORONDESIGN.COM" and the page number twice. These are omitted below.

Skipped: **p.49** is the chapter title page ("Y AXIS"). **p.76** is a trivia page ("The first design released under the name VORON was the “VORON Geared Extruder”, on January 28, 2015.").

## p.71 — LINEAR RAILS
Shows: An MGN9 rail with its carriage. M3 screws and T-nuts are drawn in every other hole.
Hardware labels: none.
Tips:
- HANDLE WITH CARE: The carriage can slide off the rail if not handled properly. Dropping the carriage will likely damage it. Any marks, dents or nicks might cause the linear rail to misbehave in operation.
- LINEAR RAILS - PREPARATION AND MOUNTING: Most linear rails arrive coated in shipping oil. To ensure smooth motion and a long service life, this oil must be removed and the rail carriage re-greased. See the VORON sourcing guide for a list of recommended lubricants. We've also included a link to a video guide to help you get started. When designing this printer's mounting pattern, we opted to skip every other mounting hole on the linear rails. This reduces hardware count while still meeting the requirements of our use case. If your rails have an even number of mounting holes, work from the outside inward and double up on the center screws. When tightening, work from the center outward to ensure the rail sits flush against the extrusion.
Other text: QR code, https://voron.link/agu0nes

## CAD vs manual

- **Which Y extrusion comes first (pp.51/52):** the p.51 thumbnail, viewed from the front right, highlights the right extrusion. In the CAD the right Y extrusion has T-nuts at y ≈ 34 (front, top and bottom) and at y ≈ 353 and 369 (back, top and bottom), 6 in total. The left extrusion has the same 6. They come from `Gantry_Frame_Tnuts` and are matched by position.
- **Post T-nuts (p.53):** the CAD has them in the post's back-facing slot (y = 6.4), at z −142 and −178, above and below the Y extrusion. These are `Gantry_Frame_Tnuts` M5:42/45 (right) and M5:53/55 (left).
- **Idler screws:** the 4 M5x10 per idler are in the CAD under each `Front Idler` subtree (`Front_Idler_A_Hardware`, for both idlers).
- **A drive screws (p.58, "four M5x10"):** the CAD places both an M5x10 (`M5x10 BHCS (4):8`) and an M5x16 (`M5x16 BHCS (4):6`) at the rear-bottom position, (0, 371, −171). The app draws the endstop-pod option, so it shows 3 M5x10 on p.58 and the M5x16 through the pod on p.63. The M5x10 is the sensorless alternative and is not drawn. A tip on the p.58 step explains this.
- **B drive missing screw (p.59):** the CAD has 4 screws on the B drive's Y extrusion. The one left out is the rear-bottom screw (−390, 371, −171), `B_Drive_Hardware:1/M5x10 BHCS (1):8`. It mirrors the A drive's pod position, and the 250 dpi render of p.59 shows the front one of the two bottom holes filled. I could not find the "later step" that installs it, so it is not drawn yet.
- **Endstop options (pp.60–63):** the CAD holds both the pod (with the D2F-L switch and 2 M2x10) and the bumper (`[a]_y_Bumper` with an M3 heat set insert and M3x12 SHCS) in the same place under the A drive. The manual's main option, the pod, is drawn. The bumper's M3 heat set insert is the "third M3 insert" the pilot set aside.
- **Endstop screw direction:** p.63 shows the M5x16 from the drawing's top, which is the underside of the upright printer. In the CAD the screw points up into the bottom of the Y extrusion, through the pod.
- **D extrusion:** `HFSB5-2020-240-D`, 240 mm, at y = 386. Its 10 T-nuts match p.64: x ≈ −307/−291 and −99/−83 at the top and bottom of each end, and x ≈ −219 and −171 in the bottom slot. The two middle ones take the upper braces' vertical screws.
- **Upper braces (pp.67/68):** the CAD names them `Rear_VertExt_Upper_Brace_Right` (x ≈ −173) and `_Left` (x ≈ −217). The page views don't show clearly which comes first. The app fits the right brace on the p.67 step and the left one on p.68. The 2 H T-nuts (p.67) come from the shared `Frame_Hardware/T-Nuts` pool: M5:32/33 at (−188.6 / −201.4, 390, −188).
- **Brace kind:** neither brace file has the `[a]_` prefix, so both are primary. The manual draws no heart on them.
- **Y rails:** in the CAD the rails sit under the Y extrusions (z ≈ −173), which is the up-facing side once the printer is flipped (p.70). 8 M3 T-nuts and 8 M3x8 SHCS per rail, spaced 40 mm, as on pp.72–75. The CAD rail is a single solid with no separate carriage.
- **Rail order:** the p.75 bottom view shows the endstop pod at the drive end, so pp.72–73 are taken as the left (B) rail and pp.74–75 as the right (A) rail.
- **Rail T-nut M3:54:** the `Gantry_Frame_Tnuts` M3:54 at (0, 315, −154), in the top slot of the right Y extrusion, holds the Y cable chain's unfixed end (`10x11 Chain Y` M3x6 FHCS:10). It is not part of this chapter.
- **Sub-assemblies onto the frame:** the idlers and drives travel as their roots (housing, carriers and front; upper, lower and motor). The pilot's bench-base parts (idler carrier A, drive upper halves) take plain movers, because `build-data.cjs` refuses `again` for a part no earlier step installed.
