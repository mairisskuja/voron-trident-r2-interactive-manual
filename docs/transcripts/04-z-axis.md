# Voron Trident Assembly Manual: Z Axis transcript, pp. 77–117

Source: `Assembly_Manual_Trident.pdf` (VoronDesign, GPL-3.0, version 2026-06-24). Tip titles and texts are verbatim from `pdftotext -layout`, with line wraps joined and ligatures written out. The descriptions of the illustrations are my own, based on renders at 1000 px width. The conventions are the same as in [the pilot transcript](../pilot-transcript.md): blue pills are labels, light-blue fill marks new parts, and a red heart marks an accent part.

Module: `tools/chapters/04-z-axis.cjs` (31 steps).

## p.78 — OVERVIEW
Shows: an isometric view of the frame with the three Z assemblies in light blue, labelled "Z Rear", "Z Left" and "Z Right". Each assembly is a rail, a motor mount and motor at the bottom, and a Z joint on the carriage. On the right are a top view and a front view. Orientation labels: Top, Back, Front.
Labels: Z Rear, Z Left, Z Right. Tips: none. This page is not a step.

## p.79 — Z-AXIS PARTS PREPARATION
Shows: two cable chain insert plates. The "generic" plate takes 3 M3 heat set inserts, in the lower three holes. The "IGUS" plate takes 2, in the lower two holes. On the right, the rear Z joint (z_carriage_rear) takes 2 M3 heat set inserts in its side face.
Hardware labels: "M3 Heat Set Insert" (×2 pills).
Tips:
- GENERIC CABLE CHAIN: The 3 hole pattern is usually found on generic cable chains.
- IGUS CABLE CHAIN: IGUS chain have 2 mounting holes.
- CABLE CHAIN MOUNTING PATTERN: Multiple mounting patterns are available for cable chains on the market. Pick the part that matches with the hole pattern on your mounting chain. The remainder of the manual will only show the “generic” pattern.

## p.82 — Z AXIS - REAR
Shows: two M5 T-nuts in the C extrusion, either side of the H (vertical brace) foot. On the right, the rear motor mount (light blue) sits on them against the brace. The thumbnail highlights the C extrusion.
Hardware labels: "M5 T-Nut". Tips: none.

## p.83 — Z AXIS - REAR
Shows: a front view of the mount with 2 M5x16 BHCS going in from the front, left and right. On the right, a printed alignment tool (light blue) bridges the mount and the brace.
Hardware labels: "M5x16 BHCS".
Tips:
- SQUARE IT UP: Use the alignment tool to bring the rear Z motor mount into alignment with the vertical brace, then snug up the M5 screws. Make sure the top surface of the extrusions and the printed part are flush.

## p.84 — Z RAILS
Shows: a rail with its carriage sliding off the bottom end, crossed out with a big red X.
Tips:
- RAIL SAFETY: Mind the rail carriage during installation. If your rails were delivered with plastic stoppers you can also temporarily reinstall them to prevent carriages from falling off their rails and spilling their bearing balls.
Other text: "For illustrative purposes only. Do not attempt to recreate this scene. No actual carriages were harmed in the making of this image."

## p.85 — Z AXIS - REAR
Shows three panels:
1. 8 M3 T-nuts in the H extrusion. The top and bottom ones are circled.
2. The MGN9 rail with its carriage at the top.
3. 8 M3x8 SHCS.

An inset shows a small U-shaped printed guide.
Hardware labels: "M3 T-Nut", "MGN9 Rail", "M3x8 SHCS".
Tips:
- T-NUT ORIENTATION: Insert the t-nuts as shown in the highlight.
- CENTERED RAIL INSTALLATION GUIDE: Use the MGN9 guides to position the rail in the center of the extrusion prior to fastening the screws.

## p.86 — Z AXIS - REAR
Shows: the top of the rail circled where it meets the brace, and a circle at the bottom of the rail just above the motor mount.
Tips:
- SEAT AGAINST THE BRACE: Push the linear rail until it seats against the vertical brace.
- BOTTOM GAP: With the rail seated against the top brace, a small gap should remain here to clear the cover part.

## p.87 — Z AXIS - FRONT LEFT
Shows: 2 M5 T-nuts in the front-left bottom corner, one in each bottom extrusion, both circled. On the right, the left motor mount (light blue) sits on them in the corner, with its arrow circled. The thumbnail highlights the front-left post.
Tips:
- T-NUT ORIENTATION: Insert the t-nuts as shown in the highlight.
- ARROWS FRONT: Orient the part so the arrow points to the front frame extrusion.

## p.88 — Z AXIS - FRONT LEFT
Shows: 2 M5x16 BHCS going into the mount's side slots. The right panel shows the result.
Hardware labels: "M5x16 BHCS".
Tips:
- KEEP IT FLAT: Make sure the top surfaces of the extrusions and the printed part sit on the same plane. This keeps the motor, leadscrew, and linear rail properly aligned.

## p.89 — Z AXIS - FRONT LEFT
Shows: the rail stop (an accent part, red heart) and its position, circled, directly under the front idler in the post channel.
Tips:
- EASY PEASY: The rail stop clicks into the extrusion channel - no hardware required. Push it all the way up against the front idler.

## p.91 — Z AXIS - FRONT LEFT
